Hamari Kahani

Amila-Mau ke dil se, Abhinav Yadav ki soch tak.

Makhan Bhog Sweets and Pizza King, Amila-Mau mein shuddh mithai aur authentic pizza ke liye ek bharosemand naam hai. Abhinav Yadav ki aguwai mein, hum ek hi chhat ke neeche do zaikon ko jodte hain — mithai ki purani, azmayi hui parampara aur pizza ka aaj ka craze.

Har mithai khaalis ghee aur taaza mawa se, traditional vidhiyon ke saath banayi jaati hai. Har pizza order milte hi fresh banaya jaata hai — behtareen ingredients aur sahi baking ke saath.

Samosa se lekar dosa tak, hamara maqsad hamesha ek hi raha hai: Amila-Mau ke har grahak ko ek hi jagah par behtareen quality aur zaika dena.

"Mera sapna tha ki log Makhan Bhog ka naam sunte hi shuddhta aur zaika, dono yaad karein."
— Abhinav Yadav, Founder
